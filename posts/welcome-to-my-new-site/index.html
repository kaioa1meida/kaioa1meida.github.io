Welcome to my personal site & engineering blog

September 21, 2026 • 2 min read • Engineering

After maintaining my initial static portfolio for a while, I decided to rebuild it from the ground up with a clear goal in mind: creating a minimalist, blazing-fast digital space designed not only to showcase my professional profile, but also to serve as an engineering notebook for technical write-ups.

Design Philosophy

Inspired by clean, content-first typography and dark-first aesthetics, the priority here is eliminating visual noise and highlighting what truly matters:

  • Zero runtime bloat: Pure vanilla HTML and CSS without heavy framework runtimes or unnecessary dependencies.
  • Reading comfort: Balanced contrast, dark-first ground, and native light mode toggle.
  • Focused navigation: An intentional Home layout, a dedicated professional summary under /resume, and an archive of engineering thoughts under /posts.

What to Expect

I will use this blog as an open engineering journal where I share hands-on learnings, architecture decisions, and deep dives into:

  • Cloud-native backends with Kotlin, Python, and Golang;
  • Architectural patterns, microservices, and DevSecOps practices;
  • Cloud infrastructure with AWS, GCP, Kubernetes, and Terraform;
  • Reliability, observability, and audit automations at scale.
"Continuous learning is not a one-time milestone; it is the daily discipline of deliberate refinement."

Feel free to follow along with future posts or reach out via the social links in the footer!
